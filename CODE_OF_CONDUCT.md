# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Dumb UI to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- Help newcomers find their footing.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through https://1619.in or by opening a
private security advisory on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and contributions that break
this code, and may ban repeat offenders.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
